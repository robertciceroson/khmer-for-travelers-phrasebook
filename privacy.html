Privacy Policy

Khmer for Travelers Phrasebook · Effective September 27, 2026

The short version

The app collects no personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer receives nothing about you or how you use the app.

What stays on your device

Phrases you save, and the app itself for offline use, are stored only in your device's app storage. They are never sent to the developer or anyone else. Deleting the app, or clearing its data, removes them.

Voice features

  • Play in Khmer uses your device's own text-to-speech voice. The app sends nothing anywhere.
  • Speak to search and listen for a reply are optional and only run when you tap the microphone. They use your device's built-in speech recognition. On some devices that service is provided by the device maker (for example Apple or Google) and may process the audio on their servers under their own privacy policies. The app does not record, keep or send audio to the developer.

Microphone permission

The app asks for the microphone only when you use a voice feature. You can say no, or turn it off later in your device settings; everything else keeps working.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
